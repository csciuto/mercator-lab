    What's going on

    The flat map only depends on how the globe sits inside the cylinder. Turn both together and nothing changes. A copy you freeze stays printed on the cylinder and turns with it.

    Size-comparison maps keep the cylinder still and turn the globe. A country moved north lands on a part of the cylinder that stretches more, and its old latitude lines no longer match the cylinder's rings. That's where the bent borders come from.
